## Design Specificity Verdict

Genuinely authored, and now structurally honest: pin rooms equal promises exactly (measured 2700/3780/3240px, each exhausted precisely at release), the field flip is one committed 0.55s turn between y 1204-1445, the roadmap pages truthfully (one phase per 576px frame), reduced-motion renders static complete states, mobile collapses cleanly (pins become 688/2844/1418px stacked sections, zero overflow).

The lag is no longer correctness — it is velocity discipline. Every scrubbed tween is `ease: linear` on a single transform axis; the field flip is the only eased moment on the page. The page is calm, legible, and always the same speed.

Deterministic scan (B): 0 findings. One expected 404 (Vercel), no page errors, no overflow at either viewport, stacks saturate exactly at release and hold, field flips at section boundaries with no fighting tweens, reduced-motion all `none`, contrast 6.51:1 and 5.30:1.

## Overall Impression

The round-2/3 fixes closed every "the pin lies / the turn dissolves / the roadmap betrays" gap. What still lags is not wrong — it is undershot: the page is correct where the references are dense. One more moving layer per room, eased settles, and a mid-scene gesture per pin are the three gears that convert this from a trustworthy machine into an impressive one.

## What's Working

1. **The field controller** — single writer, boundary-committed, reduced-motion-aware. Better state management than either reference ships.
2. **The vine-seal-flip triple-beat in the hero** — three authored gestures in one room, every one landing before release.
3. **Pin honesty + mobile collapse** — rooms equal promises, mobile is a first-class reading experience; both documented rules, both executed exactly.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Mobile: the round-3 seal collides with the hero copy.** Measured at 390×844: the seal sits on the sub-copy's last line ("…implemented, tested, and understood." reads behind it). Fix: `bottom-60 right-8` → `bottom-72 right-8`, or cap the sub at max-w-[85%]. → /impeccable adapt

**[P1] Monophonic velocity.** Every scrubbed Animation runs `ease: linear` (the only defaults in lib/scrollytelling.tsx:120,188); the field flip is the single eased moment on the page. The reference dynamism is exactly layered velocities with eased resolves. Fix: plumb `ease` through Animation (already supported) — `power2.out` on the vine settle, `sine.inOut` on the tier stack — and add a second velocity layer per pin. → /impeccable animate

**[P2] The tier pin's 4,200px heart is one constant-rate slide.** Two stamp moments bracket the biggest room; nothing resolves between them. Fix: make the stake rail move at its own slower speed against the plate, and add a mid-pin beat (a masked context reveal at 50%, a grape-cluster swell at 75%). → /impeccable animate

**[P2] The roadmap exit panel leaks the field.** The exit panel (bg-parchment) sits outside any field-zone Root, so the body flips to loam at the leadership boundary while the parchment panel is still on screen. Fix: wrap the exit panel in its own zone-rooted Root with the parchment field. → /impeccable polish
