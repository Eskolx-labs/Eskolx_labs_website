## Design Specificity Verdict

Product-specific, not category-interchangeable. The growing-season narrative drives structure (seed to sprout to roots to trellis to seasons to keepers to harvest) and the signature mechanic (scroll-scrubbed L-system vine). Color, type, texture derive from one pinned brief; the Eshcol root, trellis, keepers, harvest all carry product truth. Only tissue at the seams: social link grid, "Follow the code..." line, dry nav labels next to the almanac's own names.

Deterministic scan: 15 findings, all advisory, one family: 14x design-system-font-size (15px/13px off the documented ramp; 0.8rem in dead ui/button.tsx) + 1x design-system-radius (2px focus ring). None functional; ramp documentation drift.

Browser evidence: 0 broken anchors, 0 missing alt, h1 single, CTA tab-reachable, no horizontal overflow on desktop or mobile. Console: favicon.ico 404, _vercel/insights 404 (non-Vercel prod build, harmless), and GSAP "target [data-seal] not found" (the footer seal animation never runs — SealMark does not forward the data-seal prop).

## Cognitive Load

Checklist: single focus partial-fail, chunking pass, grouping pass, mobile hierarchy fail, one-thing-at-a-time pass, minimal choices fail (hero 7 options, community 9), working memory pass (X Factor maps 2 of 3), progressive disclosure pass. Band: moderate.

## Emotional Journey

Strong hero peak. Valley: the entire middle is a syllabus tour with no rising action. End: the harvest fizzles into an 8-link directory, saved by the footer seal press. Reassurance at key moments is good.

## What's Working

1. L-system signature: deterministic SSR-safe seed, draw order interleaving depth, fruit after wood, back.out pops, reduced-motion degradation.
2. Parchment plate + vermilion seal in Origin is the page's one true material shift, the moment the almanac becomes real.
3. Component discipline: one leaf, one berry, one plate grammar, tabular numerals, press-state buttons.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Mobile loses the signature: vine is lg-gated everywhere; Casey's page is identical stacked sections with no growing vine.
- [P1] Tier tabs remount and re-animate the plate on every click: flash + layout shift; role=tablist has no arrow-key navigation, no aria-controls.
- [P2] CTA desert in the persuasion spine: only hero, nav, and bottom carry conversion; the middle seven sections are a museum.
- [P2] Copy/link hygiene: "MIT License" and "Community Guidelines" link to the org page, not the license; "X Factor" maps 2 of 3 pillars; "Dark Earth" card is meta-copy breaking the world.
- [P2] Contrast floor on small print: cream-400 at 11-13px mono is ~4.4:1 against loam, attenuated by the grain overlay; use cream-200 below 14px.
- [P3] GSAP seal tween targets a prop never forwarded; glyphs float illegibly on the vine; favicon 404.
