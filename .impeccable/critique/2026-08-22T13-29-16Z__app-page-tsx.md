## Design Specificity Verdict

A genuinely designed page, not a themed one: almanac concept enforced at every layer (body fields, hand-authored vine, one vermilion, hairline grammar). Concept discipline exceeds the references; motion vocabulary is narrower — vertical translate, scale-in, color swap, one flip, all `ease: linear`. The page is calm and correct, but the impression moments are small (an 80px seal, a background flip, a vine rising to 1.0). Basement's impression is the whole stage; Eskolx's is a prop.

Round-1 deltas verified live: hero beats now cover the room (never silent), field flip is one 0.55s power2.inOut commit (no dissolve), seal stamps at 58-75%, vine rises 0.6→1.0 and gilds. All confirmed on the fresh build.

Deterministic scan (B): 0 findings. No page errors, one expected 404, no overflow at 1440/390, contrast 6.51:1 and 5.30:1 (AA).

## Overall Impression

The architecture is now at the references' level — scrub-by-timeline rooms, single-writer field controller, pins with real choreography, graceful reduced-motion. The gap to basement/minimal-goods is no longer structural; it is authored motion: velocity, typographic reflow, a fullscreen gesture, a real finale. Those four fixes are composable on the existing discipline; nothing about the skeleton needs to change.

## What's Working

1. The two-field page with the single-writer field controller — production-grade thinking, the round-1 fix, excellent.
2. The vine — hand-authored, currentColor-wired, rises from 0.6 scale into the field turn and gilds as the page goes loam. The best fusion of story and motion on the page.
3. Structural honesty — tier pin pays its room (4 pages ending dead-on at 100%), every tween killed and reverted, reduced-motion handled in Root, Lenis, and the field controller.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The roadmap pin lies to the reader.** Phase stack = 3 cards × 274px = 822px inside an 823px window — all three "pages of the method" are on screen at pin start, so there is nothing to page; the -66.7% scrub then pushes the stack out entirely, leaving the last ~25% of the pin a dead plate. Fix: make each phase fill the window and scrub a real page turn, or cut the room. → /impeccable animate

**[P1] The pinned room is 1/3 longer than the pin can hold.** Pin(300vh) + sticky h-screen holds exactly 200vh (66%); tiers 76%; roadmap 72%. Timelines run 0-100 over the full room, so every section's last quarter is choreographed while unpinned. Normalize every timeline to end at `room - 100vh`, then compose a deliberate exit run. This is the mechanical root of "not dynamic enough". → /impeccable polish on lib/scrollytelling.tsx Root

**[P2] The hero headline is a wipe, not a reveal.** Uniform linear translateY(-110%) through masks; "Abundant" never gets its own moment. Character-split opacity wash on the em tail, stagger the windows, add a settle hold before the mask consumes each line. → /impeccable animate

**[P2] Mobile inherits none of the theater.** The vine, the seal, and the golden column are desktop-only (hidden lg); at 390x844 the hero is lines + copy + buttons + a fading cue. Give mobile its own seal/vine beat. → /impeccable adapt

**[P3] Stake chips light early.** Chips fire at 0/25/50/75 but card-tops arrive at 0/21.7/43.3/65; the rail announces a tier while its card is still below the fold. Retarget STAKE_AT to card-top math. → /impeccable animate

**[P3] Two timing systems own the same knob.** globals.css sets `transition: background-color 0.4s linear` on body while the field controller GSAP-tweens `--field-*` (0.55s power2.inOut). No conflict observed, but it is two brains on one limb; remove the CSS transition.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan (exacting owner):** the finale is a static grid of social icons — the story's last screen is a directory, not a decision. If the climax is the loam turn, the reader exits at the book's index. Also: the roadmap "paging" is a lie the audience can feel, on the page that sells "we rebuild from scratch."
- **Riley (engineer/animator):** every tween is `ease: linear` from timeline defaults; only motion styling is scatter. Velocity, overshoot, arcing, parallax-with-drag — none exist. The work is competent, not felt.
- **Casey (conversion-minded):** the two CTAs arrive once at 62% of a hero, once mid-page, once at the end; the most valuable real estate (post-flip loam) is a quiet exit. "Join" should ride the loam moment, not ghost it.

## Minor Observations

- Scroll cue says "Scroll to turn the page" but anchors to #ecosystem ~6 screens below the actual turn.
- heroHead drifts yPercent 6 over 100% — imperceptible at 300vh; bump to 12-14% or kill it.
- Footer wraps a Root with a same-field no-op zone — harmless, but adds a zone for nothing.
- The logo 1.9→1 scrub is a nice detail; the mobile menu reuses the field system well.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the almanac is the brand, why does the harvest table (the finale) look like a social link directory? What if the end closes the book — one last paper flip back to parchment, with the CTA as the last entry?
2. The seal is the brand's signature — why is it 80px, off-center, and desktop-only? What does your signature feel like next to minimal-goods' fullscreen circle?
3. If you could keep only one pin (tiers or roadmap), which is the "method" of the lab — and is the roadmap even needed as a 3.6vh experience?
