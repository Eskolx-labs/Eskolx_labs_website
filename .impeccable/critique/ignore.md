# Critique ignore list

Accepted house devices of the committed almanac world. The browser detector
flags these on rendered DOM; they are intentional, reviewed decisions, not
slop. Re-flagging them in future critiques wastes a round.

## gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow (7 hits)

Framed plates carry `border-parchment-ink/20` + tight negative-spread press
shadows (`0_24px_60px_-30px`). This is pressed-paper material, not floating
card syndrome: shadows never glow, every plate is load-bearing content, and
the grammar is identical across all chapters. Keep.

## oversized-h1 (1 hit: hero, ~74px, 3 lines)

The cover page's display headline at the top of its clamp range is the
almanac frontispiece. Scale IS the hierarchy here. Keep.

## kicker-above-heading (2 hits), wide-tracking, all-caps-body

Mono micro-labels ("The name", "The field guide", "OPEN SOURCE · MIT
LICENSE · EVERYTHING PUBLIC") with 0.12–0.22em tracking are the house
eyebrow device, always ≤11px mono uppercase, never body prose. Keep.

## nested-cards (4 hits)

Layered paper is deliberate: code terminals inside tier plates, FAQ rows
inside the ledger frame, do/don't plates inside the ruled gap-px grid.
Each layer earns separation the flat alternative can't. Keep.

## cramped-padding (1 hit: field-guide requirements wrapper)

Measured artifact — the flagged `div.mt-7` wrapper sits inside a plate
with p-7/p-9 inset; the wrapper itself needs none. False positive.
