## Design Specificity Verdict

The almanac is a real, specific, disciplined world — the strongest single asset on the page. Eight field zones verified committing cleanly (hero paper→loam mid-pin, roadmap loam→paper, leadership paper→loam, community loam→paper, exit panel and footer hold paper — the R4 parchment fix works). The system is correct; the performance is too small, too linear, and too quiet between events. The biggest single gap: all scrubbed tweens are `ease: linear` with no settle time, the seal stamps without a thump, and the mobile fold ships the signature beats already fully resolved.

Deterministic scan (B): 0 findings. No page errors, one expected 404, no overflow at either viewport, stacks saturate exactly at release, stake click lands at 5400 (target 5400.25 — the jump works), mobile seal/sub no overlap, reduced-motion all `none`, contrast 6.51:1 and 5.30:1.

## Overall Impression

The kinematics (pins, field flips, reveals, dots, vine) are correct and beautifully maintained, but the rhythm is one speed everywhere (linear, 1:1 scrub), the two signature beats (field turn + seal stamp) fire only twice each across 12,942px, and the mobile fold is a static still. The page reads like a printed almanac with a scroll handle; the references read like a performance. Fix the P0 + the ease/settle commands and the motion will outclaim both references — the system and its authority already do.

## What's Working

1. **Field system ownership** — one writer, zones on every path, single 0.55s commit at every boundary. Eight zones probed; zero races, zero leaks. Better engineering than most reference sites.
2. **Truthful pin bookkeeping** — hero 300vh, tiers 420vh (room 2880 = 4×720 scroll-quarters; stakes light exactly at 25/50/75), roadmap 360vh with a perfect -66.67% stack; timeline ends at pin release.
3. **Round-4 fixes hold** — mobile seal/sub gutter 14px, exit panel holds paper, stakes are real focusable buttons that jump the pin.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] jumpToTier() uses the wrong room.** It computes `room = pin.getBoundingClientRect().height` (3780) but the scrubbed stack's room is height−viewport (2880). Stake 4 lands 675px past its tier. Fix: `room = pin height − innerHeight`, clamp target. → /impeccable polish

**[P1] The mobile fold has no signature.** Below lg there is no scrubbed timeline (correct per DESIGN.md), but the seal sits static and the vine fully risen in the first viewport. Fix: an IntersectionObserver-once, gated to max-width:1023px and no-preference, playing the same fromTo as time-based tweens (seal scale 2.2→1 back.out(1.7), vine yPercent 14→0 power3.out). → /impeccable animate

**[P1] The motion system is linear everywhere.** Every scrubbed tween uses `ease: linear`; basement's fluency comes from shape — ease-outs that finish mid-room, then the room continues past the settle. Fix: (a) seed the seal stamp with an overshoot (scale 2.6→0.86→1 so it lands and rings); (b) stretch the field flip to 1.0-1.2s power2.inOut so the turn reads as deliberate, not a strobe; (c) make tiers/roadmap scrub `scrub: 1` (they default to `true` = no lag smoothing). → /impeccable animate

**[P2] The tiers room has no ambient life and a dead last quarter.** The rail and plate move at the same linear rate (no parallax split); at 100% the plate sits frozen with dead loam below. Fix: drift the rail y:0→12 over the room (differential parallax), light each stake a beat early, breathe the last quarter with a slow gold shimmer or a tier-4 tag fade at 75%. → /impeccable animate

**[P2] Roadmap's last quarter is static.** The 3rd phase sits frozen for the final ~22% of the room. Fix: move the exit panel's entrance into the pin's tail (y:24→0, opacity 0→1 at 88-100 of the same Root) so the release pays for the room. → /impeccable animate
